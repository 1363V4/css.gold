- the golden manifesto -

in the beginning, there was nothing.

and then there was light. information. craft.

and then came dark patterns. infinite scrolls. spectacle.

no more.

this manifesto is a call to arms.

to strip away the unnecessary, leaving only what matters.

a rallying cry for designers, developers, and users who believe in a better web.

a return to flat design is not regression.

it is evolution.

i. flat beauty

abolish shadows.

eradicate gradients.

embrace the purity of solid colors and clean lines.

depth is deception.

flat is true.

ii. fill screen

every pixel matters.

use space with purpose and precision.

no dead zones. no wasted potential.

the screen is your canvas.

iii. don't scroll

scrolling is submission to poor design.

scrolling is the only swipe with no intent.

nothing should lurk outside the viewport.

navigate, don't excavate.

iv. simple as

minimize clicks.

maximize efficiency.

every interaction should be intuitive, every path clear.

complexity is weakness.

simplicity is power.

v. no spill

content must be contained, controlled, curated.

no more bottomless pits of information.

no more splash spills of slop.

boundaries, not endless voids.

know where to cut.

vi. function first

beauty lies in utility.

design should serve the user, not somebody else's ego.

if it doesn't enhance usability, it doesn't belong.

if you're not proud of it, don't make it.

give what you want to have.

vii. draw grids

the grid is our foundation.

the grid groups and separates, grows and shrinks.

show relations, keep it relative.

structure is salvation.

viii. go fast

every second of load time is stolen from the user.

every second of dev time should be spent with intent.

flat design is fast design.

optimize relentlessly.

ix. stay accessible

accessibility is not a luxury but a necessity.

information has no shape, no limits, no conditions.

yes, it's your website fault if it can't get through.

design for all, no exceptions.

x. resist trends

we stand firm against a stream of fads.

restless compasses are the enemies of efficiency.

perfection is achieved when there's nothing to remove.

fight bloat, and never think short term.

build to last.

here are the rules of golden design.

here are our 10 golden commandments.

stop the spill.

claim back the screen.

claim back beauty.
